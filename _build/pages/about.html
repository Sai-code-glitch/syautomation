Our story

Why SYAUTOMATION exists.

Most small business owners we meet didn't start a business to copy data between apps, chase invoices or answer the same five questions all day. Yet that's where their evenings go.

The tools to fix this are now cheap and powerful: workflow platforms, AI assistants, WhatsApp and SMS APIs. What's missing is someone to set them up properly, connect them to the way the business really works, and keep them running when things change.

That's what we do. We're a remote studio, so we can work with a dental clinic in Manchester, a salon in Dubai and an online store in Sydney in the same week, each at a fair local price.
