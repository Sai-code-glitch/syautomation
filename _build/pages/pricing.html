Billing FAQ

Questions about pricing.

What does the monthly care plan cover?

Monitoring, fixes when an app changes, a set number of hours of improvements each month, and a monthly results report.

Is there a minimum contract?

The care plan has a three-month minimum so we can prove results. After that it's month to month, and you keep everything we built if you leave.

Who pays for the software?

You pay software subscriptions directly in your own accounts, so you always own them. Most clients spend between 20 and 120 US dollars a month on tools. We recommend the cheapest option that does the job.

How do I pay?

By card, bank transfer, PayPal, Wise, or UPI and Razorpay in India. Invoices are issued in your currency.

Do prices include tax?

Prices exclude VAT, GST and sales tax. We add tax where the law requires it, based on your country.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade any time and we only charge the difference in setup. Downgrades take effect at the next billing month.
