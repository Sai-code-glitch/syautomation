FAQ

Questions clinics ask us.

Does the AI receptionist work with my booking system?

In most cases, yes. We connect to common calendars and practice booking tools. If your system has no way to connect, the assistant takes the booking request and sends it to your front desk instantly.

What happens with urgent or clinical questions?

The assistant never gives medical advice. It answers only from information you approve, and passes anything urgent or uncertain to a person straight away.

How much does it cost?

Most clinics choose the Growth package: 2,400 US dollars one-time setup plus 399 US dollars a month for the care plan. Prices are adjusted for your country, and the free audit tells you exactly what you'd need.

Is patient data kept private?

Accounts and data stay in your clinic's name, we sign a data processing agreement, and we choose data regions that fit your local rules. See our Security page for details.

How much can reminders reduce no-shows?

Automated reminders with easy rebooking typically cut no-shows by 30 to 50 percent. We measure your before-and-after numbers in the monthly report.
