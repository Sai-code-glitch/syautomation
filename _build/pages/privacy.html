1. Who we are

{{legalName}} ("SYAUTOMATION", "we", "us") provides automation and AI services to businesses. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website. You can reach us at {{email}}.

When we build automations for a client, the client is the data controller for their customers' data and we act as a processor under a written data processing agreement.

2. What we collect

  • Details you give us: name, email, phone, business name, country, team size and anything you write in our forms, chat or emails.
  • Booking details: the call time you choose and your time zone.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location from IP address, collected only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Client project data: logins and data needed to build your automations, handled under our data processing agreement.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and arrange your automation audit.
  • To deliver, support and invoice our services.
  • To send our monthly newsletter, only if you subscribe.
  • To improve our website, only with your analytics consent.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

4. Legal bases (GDPR and UK GDPR)

We rely on contract to deliver services, legitimate interests to reply to business enquiries and keep our services secure, consent for newsletters and non-essential cookies, and legal obligation for tax and accounting records. You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business, such as hosting, email, scheduling, payments and AI providers, under contracts that protect your data. Our current providers are listed on our Security page. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

6. International transfers

Because we work worldwide, data may be processed outside your country. Where data leaves the EU, UK or another region with transfer rules, we use safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. California residents can ask what we collect and ask us to delete it, and we don't sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising. Under India's DPDP Act you may also nominate someone to exercise your rights.

To use any of these rights, email {{email}}. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the ICO in the UK or your EU supervisory authority.
