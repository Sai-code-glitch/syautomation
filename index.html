    FAQ

    Questions owners ask us.

    Something else on your mind? Ask on your free audit call or use the chat in the corner.

    Do I need to be technical?

    No. We do the setup, testing and training. You approve the plan, give us access to your tools, and we handle the rest.

    Will I have to change the software I use?

    Usually not. We connect the tools you already have, such as Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, WhatsApp, Shopify, QuickBooks or Xero. If something is missing, we recommend an affordable option.

    Do you work with businesses in my country?

    Yes. We work remotely with businesses worldwide, across time zones, and bill in eight currencies. Chat assistants can answer in your customers' languages.

    Who owns the automations and data?

    You do. Everything is built in accounts registered to your business. We sign a data processing agreement and follow GDPR and local privacy laws.

    Will AI say the wrong thing to my customers?

    Assistants only answer from information you approve, and hand the conversation to a person when they aren't sure. You can read every conversation.
